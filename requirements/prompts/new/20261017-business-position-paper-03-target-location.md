# Prompt: Position Paper 03 - Target Location

## Requirement Reference

- **Discovery Plan:** `requirements/discovery/position-papers/03-target-business-location.md`

## Objective

Recommend where detailed location validation should begin after considering the
selected customer, operating model, inputs, outputs, costs, regulation, labor,
infrastructure, and resilience.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Mandatory location gates are separated from weighted preferences.
2. Region, metro, municipality, and property are evaluated separately.
3. Customer, material, equipment, building, labor, utility, tax, regulation,
   freight, and risk assumptions are modeled.
4. A shortlist and property-level validation plan are produced.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Location thesis and sensitivity analysis.

### Out of Scope

- Final property commitment.

## Technical Notes

- Use actual quotes before final recommendation.

## Testing Requirements

- [ ] Verify data dates, geography, and scenario comparability.

## Dependencies

- Position Papers 01 and 02 plus location discovery topics.
