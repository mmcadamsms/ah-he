# Prompt: Position Paper 03 - Target Location

## Requirement Reference

- **Position Paper Plan:** `requirements/position-papers/03-target-business-location.md`

## Objective

Select and answer the location question packet implied by the chosen customer,
market-entry model, and first capability, then recommend where detailed
location validation should begin.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The prerequisite canonical location questions are answered in their topic
   files with current, comparable, location-specific evidence.
2. Mandatory location gates are separated from weighted preferences.
3. Region, metro, municipality, and property are evaluated separately.
4. Customer, material, equipment, building, labor, utility, tax, regulation,
   freight, and risk assumptions are modeled.
5. A shortlist and property-level validation plan are produced.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Location thesis and sensitivity analysis.

### Out of Scope

- Final property commitment.

## Technical Notes

- Use actual quotes before final recommendation.

## Testing Requirements

- [ ] Verify data dates, geography, and scenario comparability.

## Dependencies

- Position Papers 01, 02, and 04 plus an answered location question packet.
