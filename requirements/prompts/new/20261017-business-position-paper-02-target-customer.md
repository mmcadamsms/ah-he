# Prompt: Position Paper 02 - Target Customer

## Requirement Reference

- **Discovery Plan:** `requirements/discovery/position-papers/02-target-customer-story.md`

## Objective

Define the initial direct-to-customer target, customer problem, offer, journey,
trust model, and reason to buy.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. One primary and one secondary target customer are selected.
2. The initial offer and exclusions are explicit.
3. The full customer journey and trust evidence are defined.
4. Paid market tests and stop conditions are recommended.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Customer segment, problem, buying process, experience, trust, and channels.

### Out of Scope

- Assuming direct-to-customer means only consumer retail.

## Technical Notes

- Separate customer assertions from tested behavior.

## Testing Requirements

- [ ] Validate through interviews, quote requests, or paid experiments.
