# Financial Model and Capital Readiness Questions

## Purpose

Questions needed to determine whether the proposed business can survive,
finance its learning, and earn an acceptable return. This topic complements
tax research by focusing on integrated cash, capital, unit economics, and
downside decisions.

These are discovery questions, not approved requirements. Question IDs are
permanent and canonical in this file.
