# Sales and Go-to-Market Questions

## Purpose

Questions about finding, qualifying, winning, onboarding, retaining, and
expanding customers. This topic turns a selected market and customer story into
a measurable commercial system rather than assuming demand will arrive after
capability is built.

These are discovery questions, not approved requirements. Question IDs are
permanent and canonical in this file.
