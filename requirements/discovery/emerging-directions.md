# Emerging Directions

## Purpose

These topics have partial direction from prior conversations and requirements,
but they still need continued validation. They are not substitutes for formal
requirements or completed discovery answers.

- The intended business is a small machine and metal job shop with an
  automation-first operating philosophy.
- The stated values include honoring God and family and creating opportunities
  to employ family.
- AI is expected to help a small organization perform beyond the scale
  normally implied by its headcount.
- The business thesis includes US manufacturing reshoring as AI, automation,
  and robotics reduce the importance of low-cost labor arbitrage.
- Customer visibility throughout the process is a core product principle.
- Reproducibility should progress from a high-level history to full production
  evidence, potentially including NC code, process logs, images, and video.
- Physical setups must be derived from geometry, accessibility, workholding,
  and customer-approved contact constraints rather than a fixed setup count.
- Automatic machine indexing is not the same as a physical re-clamp.
- Simulation timing must derive from generated G-code and the machine profile.
- Demonstration G-code must not be represented as production-safe.
- Assumptions must be visible and distinguished from customer-provided facts.
