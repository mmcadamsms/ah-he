# Physical-Idea Automation Business Question Backlog

## Purpose

This is a living, free-flow list of questions that must be answered over time
to turn the business idea into a safe, useful, commercially viable service.

The vision is to help a customer move from an idea or problem statement to
approved physical parts or assemblies through requirements discovery, design,
engineering, manufacturing, finishing, inspection, assembly, packaging, and
delivery while keeping the customer informed throughout the process.

These are questions, not approved requirements. Some answers will become
product requirements, operating procedures, commercial policies, architecture
decisions, or experiments.

## How to Add to This Backlog

- Add questions under the closest topic or create a new topic.
- Append first; reorganize later if that preserves momentum.
- Use the next available identifier when practical.
- Put tentative answers under the question, clearly labeled as assumptions.
- Link answered questions to the durable document that records the decision.

## Decisions Already Emerging

The following topics have partial direction in existing requirements but still
need continued validation:

- Customer visibility throughout the process is a core product principle.
- Physical setups must be derived from geometry, accessibility, workholding,
  and customer-approved contact constraints rather than a fixed setup count.
- Automatic machine indexing is not the same as a physical re-clamp.
- Simulation timing must derive from the generated G-code and machine profile.
- Demonstration G-code must not be represented as production-safe.
- Assumptions must be visible and distinguished from customer-provided facts.
